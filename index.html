Why Nuwah

Furniture packages for Dubai, from one team.

Most furnishing companies in Dubai order joinery from a subcontractor and wait. Nuwah Interiors designs, builds and furnishes as one team: concept and 3D visualisation in-house, joinery from our own factory in Ajman, and a complete loose-furniture set in every tier. That is how a studio or apartment goes from empty to photographed and ready to let in ten days, at a price set upfront in AED.

Over four years we have furnished more than 400 units for homeowners, landlords and holiday-home operators, from Palm Jumeirah and Emaar Beachfront to Dubai Hills, Downtown and Saadiyat, including DTCM-compliant holiday rentals and a 6,000 sq ft office fit-out in Deira.

Common questions

How long does it take to furnish a unit?

Ten days from confirmation for a standard apartment. Because the joinery is built in our own Ajman factory, we're not waiting on subcontractors.

What's included in a furniture package?

The full loose-furniture set for every room: bed and mattress, linen, sofa, dining, curtains, carpet, lighting, artwork and styling. Standard, Premium and Lux differ in furniture grade, finishes and the feature-wall treatment. Fit-out works are quoted separately.

Do you handle DTCM holiday-home requirements?

Yes. Our household and DTCM add-on covers everything an inspection expects, from kitchenware and safe box to first-aid kit and iron board, and we furnish to holiday-home standard.

Which areas do you cover?

All of Dubai and Abu Dhabi. Recent projects include Palm Jumeirah, Emaar Beachfront, Dubai Hills, Downtown, Business Bay, Dubai South and Saadiyat.

Do you do fit-out as well as furniture?

Yes. Civil works, partitions, ceilings, flooring, HVAC and installations, with design, 3D visualisation and technical drawings from the same team, so one contractor takes the unit from concept to keys.
