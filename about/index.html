About Nuwah

We design. We build. You enjoy.

Nuwah is a luxury interior design and build company in Dubai, specialising in bespoke renovations and furnishing for personal homes, holiday rentals and residential spaces — with turnkey solutions delivered in days, not months.

Over four years we've furnished more than 400 units across the UAE, including complete DTCM holiday-home packages, and built our own joinery factory in Ajman so the whole process — concept, fit-out, furniture, styling — sits under one roof.

Founder

Halla Alfahmawi

CEO · Architect

Nuwah was founded by Halla Alfahmawi, an architect with a passion for blending design excellence with practical living solutions. With years of experience in architecture, interiors and project management, she saw a gap in the market for fast, high-quality, luxurious design-build services that serve both homeowners and property investors.

Her vision: beautiful spaces without the complexity that usually comes with interior projects. Halla leads Nuwah hands-on, and her work continues to set a new standard for residential and short-term-rental design across the region.
